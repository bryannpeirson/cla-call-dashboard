What "handled" means here
Matched every CLA ZCC Overflow call leg (queue historical detail, deduped by Call ID) to its Global engagement ID in the Contact Center call journey export. A call counts as handled only if the journey log shows it reaching CLA JP Clay's Park queue with an agent (CLA Contact Center 1) assigned. Everything else — regardless of what the queue system called it (Completed, Overflowed, Abandoned) — reached ZCC Overflow and then has no record of a live pickup. Only 2 of 334 calls across the full period met that bar.
